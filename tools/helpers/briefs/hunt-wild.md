The setting: a South Indian (Kerala, Western Ghats) forest at night, a lone walker, Asian elephants
nearby, a pack of dholes (Indian wild dogs). Find recordings of:
1. Asian elephant (Elephas maximus, NOT African if avoidable): trumpet (alarm), rumble (low,
   close), branch-breaking/feeding, heavy footfalls or moving through brush, a squeal/roar.
2. Dhole (Cuon alpinus): whistles, yaps/chatter. If none, say so (don't substitute dogs).
3. Sambar deer alarm call ("dhonk"), spotted deer (chital) alarm call, langur (Semnopithecus)
   alarm whoop.
4. Night birds of Kerala, recorded at night if possible: Indian nightjar, jungle nightjar,
   jerdon's/large-tailed nightjar, red-wattled lapwing (alarm "did-he-do-it"), common hawk-cuckoo,
   Indian scops owl, brown fish owl, mottled wood owl, spotted owlet.
5. Insects: crickets / katydids / cicadas of a tropical night, ideally Indian, long and clean.
Search with Latin and English names. xeno-canto-sourced Commons files are ideal for birds.
