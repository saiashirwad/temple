The setting: a walking sim at night in a Kerala town and forest; the player walks in sandals.
Find:
1. Footsteps, as single steps or a sequence you can cut into 6+ single steps, for each surface:
   dirt/earth path, dry leaves/forest litter, gravel, short grass, wooden planks (a hanging bridge,
   hollow), stone/laterite slab (temple courtyard), wet ground/shallow water. Walking pace, not
   running; sandals or soft shoes; dry and close-miked, little room reverb.
2. River: a medium river over rocks at night, long (60 s+), seamless-loopable if possible.
3. Forest night ambience bed, tropical, long (2 min+), without birds that are obviously
   daytime and without human noise.
4. Small-town night ambience in India (distant traffic, faint voices), long, quiet.
5. Rope/wood bridge creak; a hurricane-lantern or small fire crackle; brush rustle (body pushing
   through leaves).
Footstep packs on Freesound (CC0) are often good: search "footsteps dirt", "footsteps leaves",
"footsteps gravel", "footsteps wood", "footstep sandal".
