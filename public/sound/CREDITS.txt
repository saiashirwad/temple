Sounds in this folder are cut, filtered, noise-reduced, levelled and looped (tools/sound/cut.ts)
from field recordings and sound effects shared under open licences. Each keeps its licence;
the edited versions are shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA edits stay CC BY-SA).
calls.mp3 and steps.mp3 are sprite sheets of the clips below; forest, river, town and fire
are looped beds.
